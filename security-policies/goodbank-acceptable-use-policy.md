## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) defines the acceptable and unacceptable use
of GoodBank's information systems, devices, networks, and data. It exists to
protect GoodBank, its customers, and its employees from security risks arising
from inappropriate use of technology resources.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all GoodBank employees, contractors, consultants,
temporary staff, and any other individuals granted access to GoodBank systems
across all operating regions including:

- United Arab Emirates
- United Kingdom
- Malaysia
- European Union operations (Germany, Netherlands, Belgium, Austria, Switzerland)

## 4. General Use Principles

All users of GoodBank systems must:

- Use GoodBank systems only for legitimate business purposes
- Protect their login credentials and never share them with others
- Report any suspected security incidents or policy violations immediately
- Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and GoodBank policies
- Respect the privacy and confidentiality of customer and colleague data
- Use only approved software and applications on GoodBank devices

---

## 5. Acceptable Use

The following activities are permitted on GoodBank systems:

- Conducting legitimate GoodBank business activities
- Accessing systems and data necessary for assigned job functions
- Using approved communication tools for business correspondence
- Accessing approved external websites for work-related research
- Using GoodBank-approved cloud services for business purposes
- Participating in approved training and professional development activities

---

## 6. Unacceptable Use

The following activities are strictly prohibited on GoodBank systems:

### 6.1 Data and Information
- Accessing, copying, or sharing customer data without authorisation
- Transmitting confidential GoodBank information to unauthorised parties
- Storing sensitive data on personal devices or unapproved cloud services
- Sharing customer personally identifiable information via unencrypted channels
- Removing or destroying data without proper authorisation

### 6.2 System and Network Use
- Installing unauthorised software or applications on GoodBank devices
- Attempting to bypass or disable security controls
- Connecting personal devices to GoodBank internal networks without approval
- Using GoodBank systems to access or distribute illegal content
- Engaging in activities that consume excessive network bandwidth for non-business purposes
- Using GoodBank systems for personal financial gain or commercial activities

### 6.3 Internet and Email
- Visiting websites containing illegal, offensive, or inappropriate content
- Downloading files from untrusted sources
- Clicking links or opening attachments in suspicious emails
- Using personal email accounts for GoodBank business correspondence
- Sending mass unsolicited emails from GoodBank systems

### 6.4 Social Media
- Sharing confidential GoodBank information on social media platforms
- Making statements that could damage GoodBank's reputation
- Representing personal opinions as official GoodBank positions
- Posting customer information or internal documents publicly

### 6.5 Physical Security
- Leaving devices unattended in public or unsecured locations
- Allowing unauthorised individuals to view GoodBank screens or documents
- Removing GoodBank assets from premises without proper authorisation
- Tailgating or allowing unauthorised individuals into secured areas

---

## 7. Personal Use

Limited personal use of GoodBank systems is permitted provided it:

- Does not interfere with job performance or productivity
- Does not violate any section of this policy
- Does not consume excessive system or network resources
- Does not involve accessing inappropriate or illegal content
- Occurs outside of core working hours where possible

GoodBank reserves the right to monitor all activity on its systems regardless
of whether the activity is personal or business related.

---

## 8. Monitoring and Privacy

GoodBank monitors its systems, networks, and devices to ensure security and
policy compliance. Users should have no expectation of privacy when using
GoodBank systems. Monitoring may include:

- Network traffic analysis
- Email and communication monitoring
- Internet browsing activity
- System and application logs
- Physical access logs

All monitoring is conducted in accordance with applicable laws and regulations
across GoodBank's operating regions.

---

## 9. Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Personal devices used to access GoodBank systems must:

- Have up-to-date operating system and security patches installed
- Have approved endpoint security software installed
- Be registered with the IT Security team before accessing GoodBank systems
- Comply with GoodBank's password and encryption requirements
- Be subject to remote wipe if lost, stolen, or when employment ends

---

## 10. Remote Working

Employees working remotely must:

- Use the GoodBank approved VPN for all access to internal systems
- Ensure their home network is secured with WPA2 or WPA3 encryption
- Not work in public locations where screens may be visible to others
- Lock their device screen whenever stepping away from their workstation
- Report any security incidents immediately regardless of location

---

## 11. Policy Violations

Violations of this policy may result in:

- Immediate suspension of system access
- Disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment
- Legal action where violations involve criminal activity
- Regulatory reporting where required by applicable law
- Civil liability for damages caused by the violation

---

## 12. Reporting Violations

All suspected policy violations must be reported to:

- The Information Security team via the security incident reporting channel
- Line managers for minor violations
- HR and Legal for violations involving potential criminal activity or misconduct

Reports can be made anonymously where whistleblower protections apply.

---

## 13. Review and Maintenance

This policy will be reviewed annually or following any of the following events:

- A significant security incident involving misuse of systems
- Changes to applicable laws or regulations
- Significant changes to GoodBank's technology environment
- Changes to working practices such as new remote working arrangements
